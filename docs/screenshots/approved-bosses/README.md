# Approved boss integration evidence

Actual Chromium/SwiftShader game captures, not Blender renders. The browser suite uses a 390 × 844 touch viewport, DPR 1. `veil.png` / `veil-low.png` use the separate look-development runner at 430 × 932.

- `*-batteries-high`: closed/core-protected state with both flank targets.
- `*-core-high`: destroyed flank assemblies and exposed core.
- `*-departure-*`: early destruction, wrecks and effects.
- `veil-overload-low`: open heart at Low, without shadow or post passes.
- `veil-bays-open` and `veil-deployed-pods`: the real finite deployment schedule, advanced in the development fixture.
- `*-compound-small` / `*-compound-large`: retained finished infrastructure on all three alien worlds.

The encounter fixture freezes combat after advancing the real simulation to the requested phase. Its `pose` option advances presentation time so hinges and effects settle. This is visual regression evidence, not a claim of completing these bosses by hand or achieving phone frame-rate targets.

Final Veil images are refreshed after the flank-pod visibility correction. Authoring previews, original/runtime hashes, budgets and rollback instructions are in `art/approved-bosses/`. Check results and the deployed commit are recorded in `docs/build-status.md`.
