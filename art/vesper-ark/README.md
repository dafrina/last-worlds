# Helion Ark (Vesper sea-carrier boss)

Runtime file: `public/assets/vesper/helion-ark.glb` (967,516 bytes, sha256 `8bd2b64b00551d1286294271338ca07f075c785bc787010b0afa7b2704901b60`).
Contract: [docs/17-vesper-glass-frontier.md §3 and §5](../../docs/17-vesper-glass-frontier.md), plus the integrator brief of 15 October. The design follows the carrier in the right panel of `art/concepts/01-worlds-direction.png`: a long armoured hull, a stepped superstructure and orange window slits. The concept is a reference only; nothing is copied.

## Provenance

- All geometry, vertex colours and UVs are original and generated by `tools/blender/helion_ark.py`, using the shared pipeline in `vesper_common.py`.
- No imported meshes, third-party assets or textures.
- Built with Blender 5.2.2 LTS and its bundled glTF exporter (5.2.40), headless, on 15 October 2026.
- Deterministic: three builds were byte-identical, matching the hash above.
- **Revision 2 (15 October):** rebuilt as a long armoured warship, 7.6 × 12. Every bridge, radiator, shutter and bay socket is unchanged. The extra length is aft, so only the stern wake anchor moved: from z −3.62 to **z −7.45**.

## Rebuild

```bash
R=/home/davidfarina/Projects/alien-skies
cd /mnt/c && "/mnt/c/Program Files/Blender Foundation/Blender 5.2/blender.exe" --background --factory-startup \
  --python "$(wslpath -w $R/tools/blender/helion_ark.py)" -- \
  --glb "$(wslpath -w $R/public/assets/vesper/helion-ark.glb)" \
  --preview-dir "$(wslpath -w $R/art/vesper-ark)"
```

The build exports to a temporary file, re-parses it, and only publishes when every check passes:
- node names and exact translations (sockets);
- every node within x ±3.8 and z −7.5 … +4.5, with its top at most 3.2;
- total triangles ≤ 26,000;
- materials;
- COLOR_0 and TEXCOORD_0 with box UVs;
- the wake anchor is an empty;
- no textures, cameras, animations or extensions.

## Conventions

glTF +Y up, 1 unit = 1 game unit, **bow toward +Z** (toward the player). **y = 0 is the waterline**: the hull continues below it to the keel at y = −0.45, so a water plane at the pivot's height hides the lower hull. Every node is top-level, and its **translation is its socket**.

| | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Assembled footprint | x −3.77 … +3.77 (7.54, finned radiator sponsons included) × z −7.46 (stern) … +4.40 (bow) (11.86) |
| Height | waterline 0 to 3.15 (mast beacon); command tower top 2.82 |
| Triangles | 10,812 in total (budget 26,000) |

Materials:
- `ark_armor`: black ceramic, albedo from COLOR_0, roughness 0.32.
- `ark_trim`: pale ceramic, albedo from COLOR_0, roughness 0.38.
- `ark_glow`: orange #ff7a26, emissive; COLOR_0 is white on these faces. The runtime can drive the glow from damage.

COLOR_0 is linear and bakes ray-cast AO against the static assembly of hull, radiators and bridge, plus edge wear and soot on the wrecks. UVs are the box projection (1 UV = 1 unit).

**States:**
- Arrival / phase 1: hull, both radiators, bridge, closed bridge shutters, and bays with closed doors.
- A destroyed radiator swaps to its `_wreck` node.
- Bridge phase: slide the shutters apart; open the bay doors to launch fighters.

**Suggested hit regions** (they are the combat agent's call): radiators radius 0.75 and bridge radius 1.0, both at height 1.6.

## Previews

The lighting is Vesper's: a high, pale, cool sun and a cool sky, ACES 1.3. The ocean plane is at the waterline.
- `preview-ark.png`: phase 1 at the 55° game camera (12.5 units wide).
- `preview-ark-exposed.png`: bridge phase, with both radiators wrecked, the bridge shutters slid ±1.0 and the bay doors open ±0.9. The bridge lens and the glowing bays stay visible.
- `preview-ark-hero.png`: a 3/4 perspective.

**Known gaps:**
- Wake, spray, steam and the listing/sinking destruction are runtime effects; the wake anchor marks the stern.
- At 10.8k of the 26k budget, the remaining headroom is unspent on purpose: more detail at this scale turns into greebles that do not read on a phone.
