# Vesper craft

Runtime file: `public/assets/vesper/vesper-craft.glb` (144,884 bytes, sha256 `032740d3ff151d400ee6fd8cc064b9642321f333a3465e6fbb4b4593c22d3885`).

## Provenance

- All geometry, vertex colours and UVs are original and generated by `tools/blender/vesper_craft.py`. It uses the shared pipeline in `tools/blender/vesper_common.py`, which builds on the helpers in `khepri_infra.py` and `khepri_kit.py`.
- No imported meshes, third-party assets or textures.
- Built with Blender 5.2.2 LTS (hash `d13f752e3b9c`) and its bundled glTF exporter (`io_scene_gltf2` 5.2.40), headless, on 15 October 2026.
- Deterministic: a fixed seed and per-node `random.Random` streams. Three builds were byte-identical, matching the hash above.

## Rebuild

```bash
R=/home/davidfarina/Projects/alien-skies
cd /mnt/c && "/mnt/c/Program Files/Blender Foundation/Blender 5.2/blender.exe" --background --factory-startup \
  --python "$(wslpath -w $R/tools/blender/vesper_craft.py)" -- \
  --glb "$(wslpath -w $R/public/assets/vesper/vesper-craft.glb)" \
  --preview-dir "$(wslpath -w $R/art/vesper-craft)"
```

The build exports to a temporary file, re-parses it, and only replaces the published GLB when every check passes:
- node names and translations;
- materials;
- COLOR_0 and TEXCOORD_0 (box UVs checked per triangle);
- footprints, bases and heights;
- triangle budgets (≤ 2,500 each);
- no textures, cameras, animations or extensions.

## Conventions

glTF +Y up, 1 unit = 1 game unit. **Noses and muzzles point +Z** (toward the player), like the Khepri enemies; exhausts point −Z.
- The air craft pivot at their centre, with y = 0 as the craft mid-plane.
- The missile battery pivots at its base centre on the ground; its lowest point is y = −0.05.

Materials:
- `vesper_pale`: pale ceramic, albedo from COLOR_0, roughness 0.35.
- `vesper_ceramic`: black ceramic, albedo from COLOR_0, roughness 0.3.
- `vesper_glow`: orange #ff7a26, emissive; COLOR_0 is white on these faces.

COLOR_0 is linear and bakes ray-cast AO and edge wear. UVs are the world-scale box projection used by the Khepri kits.

**Notes:**
- **Needle:** a long pointed pale blade-fuselage with a black core ridge and orange slit, two forward-swept pale main blades, rear spikes and twin engines. It has offset fins: a dorsal fin canted left near the tail, and a ventral fin canted right further forward.
- **Diamond:** a fractured pale-ceramic diamond (plates with crack gaps) over a black core frame, with a glowing central heart and spine slits. It has two radiator wings, each a dark blade with six stacked glowing fins under a pale armour blade, plus volley guns under the wing roots.
- **Missile battery:**
  - Its plinth is the same stepped hexagon as the Vesper infrastructure emplacements, with glowing slits and a front ramp.
  - A launcher with four tubes is pitched up 22°.
  - The barrels are part of the node; rotate the whole node to aim (yaw about its pivot).
