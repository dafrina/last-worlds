# Vesper ice terrain kit

Runtime file: `public/assets/vesper/vesper-kit.glb` (540,256 bytes, sha256 `bdb730e6b115c1ac5bc5554d5d19fbb5012644f75cc99a19a942c05a1ccf9588`).
Contract: [docs/17-vesper-glass-frontier.md §1 and §3](../../docs/17-vesper-glass-frontier.md). The kit follows the right panel of `art/concepts/01-worlds-direction.png`: snowy shelves, sheer blue-white faceted ice cliffs, deep blue ocean and broken floes. The concept is a reference only.

## Provenance

- All geometry, vertex colours and UVs are original and generated by `tools/blender/vesper_kit.py`, using the shared pipeline in `vesper_common.py`.
- No imported meshes, third-party assets or textures.
- Built with Blender 5.2.2 LTS and its bundled glTF exporter (5.2.40), headless, on 15 October 2026.
- Deterministic: three builds were byte-identical, matching the hash above.
- The diorama imports the other published Vesper GLBs (`vesper-infra.glb`, `helion-ark.glb`, `vesper-craft.glb`). Its preview ground, ocean and seabed use procedural noise materials only; no Poly Haven maps were needed.

## Rebuild

```bash
R=/home/davidfarina/Projects/alien-skies
cd /mnt/c && "/mnt/c/Program Files/Blender Foundation/Blender 5.2/blender.exe" --background --factory-startup \
  --python "$(wslpath -w $R/tools/blender/vesper_kit.py)" -- \
  --glb "$(wslpath -w $R/public/assets/vesper/vesper-kit.glb)" \
  --preview-dir "$(wslpath -w $R/art/vesper-kit)" \
  --infra "$(wslpath -w $R/public/assets/vesper/vesper-infra.glb)" \
  --ark "$(wslpath -w $R/public/assets/vesper/helion-ark.glb)" \
  --craft "$(wslpath -w $R/public/assets/vesper/vesper-craft.glb)"
```

The build exports to a temporary file, re-parses it, and only publishes when every check passes: names, footprints, bases and tops (exact where marked), budgets (≤ 600 per piece; ≤ 200 for the jagged floes), materials, COLOR_0 and box UVs.

## Conventions

glTF +Y up, 1 unit = 1 game unit. Pivots are at the base centre, with the footprint centred on x = z = 0. **The seaward front of the cliffs faces +Z.**

**Materials (three):**
- `vesper_ice` (roughness 0.12): COLOR_0 carries a saturated, translucent-reading gradient. It runs from deep blue `#1d5d98` over the lower half, in the undercut and in occlusion, through `#3a87c8` and `#72b4e6`, to pale `#bfe0f5` at the top. Below the waterline it fades to an abyss blue `#0f3a66`. Revision 2 made the cliff faces more saturated and deeper blue at their feet, as in the concept.
- `vesper_snow` (roughness 0.85): snow `#e9eef3` shading to `#b9c8d8` in occlusion.
- `vesper_rock` (roughness 0.9): charcoal `#2a2d31` with AO and lighter edges.

Upward faces and ledges are snow, walls are ice, and outcrops are rock with snow caps. COLOR_0 is linear. UVs are the world-scale box projection (1 UV = 1 unit, node-local).

**Cracked sets:** each set is one parent floe split along shared jagged cracks; the pieces fit together with a gap of about 0.05.
- **Placement:** put each piece at `set_origin + R(yaw) · offset` and give every piece the same yaw R, using the same Y rotation for the offset vector as for the piece. Scale the offsets by more than 1 to let the pieces drift apart.

## Previews

The lighting is Vesper's: a high, pale, cool sun from the upper left and slightly behind, a cool sky for blue shadows, ACES 1.3. The camera is orthographic at 55° elevation with zero yaw.
- `preview-kit.png`: every land piece on a snowy shelf; bergs and floes in the ocean below the shelf edge.
- `preview-floes.png`: the jagged floes and the three cracked sets, assembled from the documented offsets.
- `preview-pack-ice.png`: pack ice at the game camera. Mixed jagged floes and brash, with cracked sets drifting slightly apart (offsets × 1.0–2.2).
- `preview-diorama.png`: the game camera (12.5 units wide). It shows:
  - a shelf at 0.8 over the sea at −1.2, its jagged edge lined with ice cliffs scaled to the 2.0 shelf height and facing outward;
  - shelf scatter: seracs, a spire, outcrops, drifts, a pressure ridge and a vent;
  - two missile sites, a cannon site, radiator rows, a cryo dome, a comm needle, a habitat and an ice hauler;
  - a pier with an icebreaker;
  - floes and bergs in the open water;
  - Needles and a Diamond at the flight plane;
  - the Helion Ark riding in the water at the top.

  It reads close to the concept's right panel, now with deep-blue cliff feet; crevasse fields and snow tracks belong to the world generator.
