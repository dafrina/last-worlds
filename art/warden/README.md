# WARDEN-1 (Luna boss)

**Current model, 20 October:** the block-armoured platform is built by `tools/blender/boss_references.py`, `build('warden')`. See [boss-references](../boss-references/README.md) for current previews, sizes and validation. The older lander description and hashes below are historical; combat sockets and descent thrusters remain unchanged.

Selected runtime file: `public/assets/luna/warden-v3-study.glb`, 10,220 triangles / 1,094,968 bytes. The previous 702,784-byte lander is archived as `warden-v2.glb`. Switch filenames in `src/render/boss-assets.ts` to roll back. Current hashes are in [the approved-boss manifest](../approved-bosses/manifest.json).
Contract: [docs/19-luna-training.md §2](../../docs/19-luna-training.md) and `src/content/warden.ts`. The sockets match the simulation: target height 1.3, pods at x ∓2.1 / z 0 (radius 0.7), and the command module at z −0.9 (radius 1.15).

**Design:** WARDEN-1 is a decommissioned **human** lunar defence lander, so it should read as human-built at a glance:
- clean white-grey panels with seams;
- yellow / black hazard striping and stencil marks;
- orange fuel tanks, blue solar wings and four splayed landing legs with footpads;
- an antenna with a dish and a beacon;
- RCS thruster quads and handrails.

Two turret pods with twin barrels flank a command module whose window band glows with reactor heat.

## Provenance

- All geometry, vertex colours and UVs are original and generated by `tools/blender/warden.py`, using the shared helpers in `tools/blender/nacre_fleet.py` and `vesper_common.py`.
- No imported meshes, third-party assets or textures.
- Built with Blender 5.2.2 LTS (hash `d13f752e3b9c`) and its bundled glTF exporter, headless, on 16 October 2026.
- Deterministic: two consecutive builds were byte-identical and match the hash above.

## Rebuild

```bash
R=/home/davidfarina/Projects/alien-skies
cd /mnt/c && "/mnt/c/Program Files/Blender Foundation/Blender 5.2/blender.exe" --background --factory-startup \
  --python "$(wslpath -w $R/tools/blender/warden.py)" -- \
  --glb "$(wslpath -w $R/public/assets/luna/warden.glb)" \
  --preview-dir "$(wslpath -w $R/art/warden)"
```

`--wip` skips publishing and writes `*-wip.png` previews.

The build exports to a temporary file, re-parses it, and only publishes when every check passes:
- node names and exact translations (sockets); no rotations;
- every mesh within x ±3.0 and z ±2.6, with its top at most 2.5;
- the hull's lowest point exactly at y −0.05;
- total triangles ≤ 14,000;
- materials;
- COLOR_0, NORMAL and TEXCOORD_0 (box UVs);
- empties for the beacon and thrusters;
- no textures, cameras, animations or extensions.

## Conventions

- glTF +Y up, 1 unit = 1 game unit, **front toward +Z** (toward the player).
- **Base pivot on the ground:** the footpad undersides are the lowest point, at y −0.05.
- Every node is top-level, and **its translation is its socket**.

## Materials

In every non-glow material, albedo = COLOR_0 (linear) × base colour, and the base colour is white. COLOR_0 bakes ray-cast AO, edge wear (lighter edges), grey regolith dust near the ground, and soot on the wrecks.

| Material | Use | Roughness / metal (authored) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `warden_hull` | White-grey panels: the deck, plating, module drum, hatches, turret boxes, leg sleeves and footpads | 0.50 / 0.25 |
| `warden_frame` | Dark grey: the frame ring, legs, struts, barrels, engine bells, rails, boom and mast. Also the black stencil and hazard stripes | 0.55 / 0.50 |
| `warden_paint` | Painted parts: orange fuel tanks, leg bands, turret bands and the module base ring, plus the yellow hazard stripes. One material carries both colours through COLOR_0 | 0.55 / 0.15 |
| `warden_solar` | Dark blue solar cells (two shades) | 0.22 / 0.55 |
| `warden_glow` | Orange-red emissive `#ff5a28` (base colour = emissive colour; COLOR_0 white). It covers the window band, the exposed reactor, the turret heat vents, the beacon, the instrument lights and the wreck embers | |

### The hull (`warden_hull`)

- **Deck and legs:**
  - An octagonal deck (centre z −0.25) with raised plating and seams, stencil marks and a hazard chevron. A hazard band runs around all eight sides, with a dark lower frame ring beneath.
  - Four descent engine bells sit under the deck.
  - Four splayed landing legs, each with a white shock sleeve and an orange band, two braces and a footpad; the front-left leg carries a ladder.
- **Pod outriggers:** sponsons with braces and hazard stripes carry the turret pods.
- **Rear equipment:**
  - A solar-wing boom with two tilted blue panels (6 × 2 cells each) on A-frames.
  - Two orange fuel tanks on cradles with white straps.
- **Front equipment:**
  - An antenna mast at the front-left, with a dish facing forward and up and the beacon on top.
  - A forward instrument pod with a camera lens and indicator lights.
- **Details:** RCS quads at four deck corners, handrails on the front diagonals, and a grey seat ring for the command module.

**States:**
- Arrival: the descent, with the thrusters firing.
- Phase 1: the hull, both pods, the core and closed hatches.
- A destroyed pod swaps to its `_wreck` node.
- Command-module phase: slide the hatches apart to expose the reactor glow.
- Death: swap the core for `warden_core_wreck` and hide the hatches.

**Suggested hit regions** (the simulation already fixes them): pods radius 0.7 and command module radius 1.15, on the 1.3 target plane.

**Known gaps:**
- Thruster flames, landing dust, the beacon blink, the turret yaw and the collapse are runtime effects.
- The body is 4.5 deep rather than the full 5.2: the deck is centred at z −0.25 around the fixed pod and core sockets, and the legs stay inside the ±2.6 envelope.
