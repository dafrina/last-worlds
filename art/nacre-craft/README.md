# Nacre craft

Runtime file: `public/assets/nacre/nacre-craft.glb` (736,152 bytes, sha256 `2814e844b76939ce84872433660dd02de1bb313deb60cd1f6878fc0584d34c08`).
Context: [docs/20-nacre-luminous-mire.md](../../docs/20-nacre-luminous-mire.md). The designs follow the ORRERY, SHARD and VEIL panels of `art/concepts/06-exotic-aliens.png` and the NACRE column of `art/concepts/05-alien-factions.png`:
- sharp sweeping crescent blades;
- layered iridescent shell plates;
- black chitin with real honeycomb cut-outs;
- teal and amber glow.

The concepts are references only; nothing is copied.

## Provenance

- All geometry, vertex colours and UVs are original and generated by `tools/blender/nacre_craft.py`.
- It uses the shared Nacre pipeline in `tools/blender/nacre_fleet.py`, which provides:
  - swept lens-section blades, crescents and open crescents with lattice windows;
  - honeycomb lattices on parametric patches;
  - framed polyhedra and segmented ring bands;
  - the iridescent bake and GLB verification.
- `nacre_fleet.py` builds on `vesper_common.py`, `khepri_infra.py` and `khepri_kit.py`.
- No imported meshes, third-party assets or textures.
- Built with Blender 5.2.2 LTS (hash `d13f752e3b9c`) and its bundled glTF exporter, headless, on 16 October 2026.
- Deterministic: a fixed seed and per-node `random.Random` streams. Two consecutive builds were byte-identical and match the hash above.

## Rebuild

```bash
R=/home/davidfarina/Projects/alien-skies
cd /mnt/c && "/mnt/c/Program Files/Blender Foundation/Blender 5.2/blender.exe" --background --factory-startup \
  --python "$(wslpath -w $R/tools/blender/nacre_craft.py)" -- \
  --glb "$(wslpath -w $R/public/assets/nacre/nacre-craft.glb)" \
  --preview-dir "$(wslpath -w $R/art/nacre-craft)"
```

`--wip` (or `--only nacre_shard,...`) skips publishing and writes `*-wip.png` previews for iteration.

The build exports to a temporary file, re-parses it, and only replaces the published GLB when every check passes:
- node names, translations (all at the origin) and socket children (plain empties at exact positions);
- materials;
- COLOR_0, NORMAL and TEXCOORD_0 (box UVs checked per triangle);
- footprints, bases and heights;
- triangle budgets (≤ 2,500 per node);
- no textures, images, cameras, animations or extensions.

## Conventions

glTF +Y up, 1 unit = 1 game unit. **Noses and muzzles point +Z** (toward the player); exhausts point −Z.
- Air craft pivot at their centre, with y = 0 as the mid-plane.
- `nacre_spore` pivots at its base centre; its lowest point is exactly y = −0.05.
- Each top-level node sits at the origin with no rotation. Its sockets are **child empties**, so a child's translation is also its model-space position.

## Materials

The loader can match material names by substring. In every non-glow material, albedo = COLOR_0 (linear) × base colour, and the base colour is white.

| Material | Use | Roughness / metal (authored) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `nacre_shell` | Iridescent violet / pearl shell plates (hulls, cheek shells, husks, inner petals, pearl shard plates) | 0.26 / 0.25 |
| `nacre_blade` | Pale silver-lilac blades (crescents, fins, petals) | 0.30 / 0.35 |
| `nacre_frame` | Black chitin: spines, lattice, clamps, cages | 0.38 / 0.30 |
| `nacre_bronze` | Dark bronze ring segments (Orrery only) | 0.42 / 0.45 |
| `nacre_glow_teal` | Emissive teal `#3ff2d8` (base colour = emissive colour; COLOR_0 white) | |
| `nacre_glow_amber` | Emissive amber `#ffa040` (base colour = emissive colour; COLOR_0 white) | |

**The iridescence is baked into COLOR_0**, because the runtime craft shader has none:
- On `nacre_shell` and `nacre_blade`, the hue follows the smoothed surface normal and position through a ramp.
- **Shell:** violet → blue → teal → blue-violet → faint pink.
- **Pearl shard plates and panes:** pastel lilac / ice blue / mint / peach / pink.
- **Blades:** silver-lilac with a faint rainbow.
- Each plate carries its own small hue offset, so neighbouring plates read as separate nacre pieces.
- Ray-cast AO and edge wear (lighter "edge" facets) are baked too.

To add a runtime thin-film sheen on top, enable it for `nacre_shell` (and optionally `nacre_blade`) faces only. Frame and glow faces must stay without it.

### `nacre_shard`: after SHARD

- **Spine:** a long, narrow black hexagonal spine ending in a sharp emitter point. Layered black vertebra chevrons run along its crest, with two thorns.
- **Plates:** six forward-pointing pearl kites float along the spine in a slight spiral, closer at the nose and wider at the tail, to give an arrow silhouette. A seventh, thin vertical shard rises above the tail.
- **Joints:** each plate is held by a black clamp node with an amber line to a glowing amber socket on the spine.
- **Glow:** amber seams between the vertebrae, plus twin amber engines.

### `nacre_veil`: after VEIL

- **Hull:** a violet seed hull with a sharp dorsal crest and a black chitin under-frame. Layered violet cheek shells sit over the blade roots, with a black keel plate carrying a teal slit.
- **Blades:** two silver crescent blades sweep from the tail out and forward to sharp tips. Each has a small open honeycomb window with a teal rim line.
- **Other parts:** forward silver barbs beside the nose, swept tail blades, and gun spines with teal tips at the blade roots. A teal engine ring is tilted back and up so it reads from the high camera.

### `nacre_orrery`: after ORRERY

- **Rings:** three nested, segmented bronze rings, kept flat so they read from above. They are tilted −8° about X, 17° about Y and 25° about X, with broad raised cap plates, sparse amber glyph slits and black joint clamps across the gaps.
- **Gimbal:** struts with bronze pivot nodes link the rings to the core.
- **Core:** a faceted amber icosahedron with a raised black frame and recessed glowing panes.
- **Emitter arms:** tapered arms with amber rings, two forward volley guns and two lateral emitters.

### `nacre_spore`: organic ground battery

- **Base:** a black chitin plinth carrying six silver sickle petals, each with a teal vein.
- **Core:** a black honeycomb lattice collar and ribs hold a framed teal core sac (the target).
- **Petals:** five violet inner petals cup the sac, with teal veins and a gap at the front for the launcher.
- **Launcher:** a black spine with teal rings and tip, pitched up 20°, held in two cradle blades. The launcher is part of the node; rotate the whole node to aim.

### `nacre_pod`: the pod THE VEIL launches

- A black seed capsule with a pointed nose.
- Two violet husk shells with sharp lateral edges wrap the flanks. Teal slits run along the spine and the flanks.
- Swept silver tail fins and a vertical dorsal fin, with a teal glow at the rear.

**Known gaps:**
- Engine plumes, the Orrery's ring spin, beams and muzzle flashes are runtime effects. The sockets mark where they go.
- The Shard's engines glow amber to match its seams; the runtime's Nacre engine-plume colour is teal. Choose per kind.
