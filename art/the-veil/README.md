# THE VEIL (Nacre boss)

Runtime file: `public/assets/nacre/the-veil.glb` (950,536 bytes, sha256 `735ec1dde15976274fba856864124f4f6839d7c7251e4d6a3d24dfd286bde490`).

**Redesign, 17 October 2026 (seed `the-veil-v2`):** the user found the first model round and animal-like ("an axolotl") and asked for something long, alien and exotic, like the fighter designs but huge. The hull is now a long, narrow faceted black warship (about 21 × 9 units, ram prow at z +7.9, stern engine block at z −14.4) with a dorsal blade spine, layered iridescent armour shards, swept knife blades (canards, solid main blades, aft blades) and crystal-crowned pods on outriggers. No tendrils or loop wings. The heart, shutters, sockets and wrecks are unchanged; the engine empties moved to the stern block. The rest of this README describes the first version where it differs.
Context: [docs/20-nacre-luminous-mire.md](../../docs/20-nacre-luminous-mire.md) and `src/content/veil.ts`. The sockets match the simulation: target height 2.2, pods at x ∓3.1 / z +0.4 with radius 0.85, the heart at z −1.8 with radius 1.2, and bays at (∓1.7, z +2.6).

**Design:** this replaces the code-built boss the user found "too round and comical". It uses the design language of the VEIL panel in `art/concepts/06-exotic-aliens.png`, with the NACRE capital ship of `05-alien-factions.png` for the top-down layout:
- a long dark-violet biomechanical seed hull with layered nacre carapace scales and a black chitin spine;
- broad crescent blades in silver and violet, layered;
- black honeycomb lattice with real cut-outs;
- teal veins and slits;
- a concealed aft propulsion ring and trailing tendrils.

There are no spheres as main forms. The concepts are references only; nothing is copied.

The build exports to a temporary file, re-parses it, and only publishes when every check passes:
- node names and exact translations (sockets);
- shutter yaw rotations (all other nodes are unrotated);
- every mesh within x ±7.5, z ±8.5 and y −0.6 … 3.4;
- total triangles ≤ 16,000;
- materials;
- COLOR_0, NORMAL and TEXCOORD_0 (box UVs);
- empties for the bays and engines;
- no textures, cameras, animations or extensions.

## Conventions

- glTF +Y up, 1 unit = 1 game unit, **bow toward +Z** (toward the player).
- **y = 0 is the mire surface.** The keel dips to y −0.48, so a water plane at the pivot hides the lower hull.
- Every node is top-level, and **its translation is its socket**.
- Only the four heart shutters carry a rotation: a yaw, explained below.

## Materials

These are the same materials as `nacre-craft.glb`; `nacre_bronze` and `nacre_glow_amber` are not used here.
- `nacre_shell`: violet hull, carapace scales, violet wing bands, shutters, iris and pod blades. Albedo from COLOR_0, with the iridescent violet → blue → teal → pink drift baked in.
- `nacre_blade`: silver-lilac blades, rims and the bay petal ring.
- `nacre_frame`: black chitin. It covers the spine, lattice, collars, cradles, tendrils, the engine shroud and the frames of the faceted cores.
- `nacre_glow_teal`: `#3ff2d8`, emissive; COLOR_0 is white on these faces. It covers the heart and gill cores, veins, slits, bay wells, the engine rings and the tendril rings. The runtime can drive it from damage or phase.

COLOR_0 is linear. It bakes ray-cast AO against the static assembly (hull, pods and heart), edge wear, and soot on the wrecks.

### The hull (`veil_hull`)

- **Seed hull:** a lofted seed hull with a crisp crest, chines and keel, tapering to a sharp prow at z +8.45. Layered nacre carapace scales run down both sides of a black chitin spine ridge, with vertebra chevrons and teal slits. Each scale is swept back to a blade-like point and has a teal seam under its trailing edge.
- **Flank lattice:** a black honeycomb panel on each flank over a teal under-glow, plus teal veins along the upper flanks.
- **Crescent blades (left/right mirrored):**
  - **Great wings:** silver, sweeping out and forward to tips at (±7.1, z +5.6), each with an open honeycomb window and teal rim lines.
  - **Rear wings:** violet with a silver leading edge, sweeping out and back to tips at (±6.0, z −8.1), each with a lattice loop.
  - **Forward scythes:** silver with a violet inner band and a teal vein, tips at (±4.25, z +8.35).
  - **Stern ribbons:** silver, tips at (±3.0, z −8.4).
  - **Prow barbs:** small, beside the prow.
  - **Seams and collars:** split-armour seams cross the silver bands, and black chitin collars sit at the blade roots.
- **Socket structures:**
  - **Pod cradles:** black cups with a silver rim on the great wings at the pod sockets, ribbed to the hull.
  - **Bays:** two launch-bay cowls at the bay sockets, each with a silver rim, five violet petal doors and an inward-facing black well with a teal floor and ring.
  - **Heart housing:** the heart socket collar, a raised black ring with a teal inner ring and twelve thorns.
- **Stern:** the aft propulsion ring, a black shroud tilted 45° back and up with a silver lip, two concentric teal rings, a recessed teal disc, a central spindle and six silver vanes. Below it, four trailing tendrils with teal rings, plus the side thrusters.

### Opening the shutters

**Placement:**
- Each shutter's translation is its **hinge midpoint** on the heart rim: radius 1.05 around the heart axis (x 0, z −1.8), at y 2.06.
- Its rotation is a pure yaw about +Y of φ = 45°, 135°, 225° and 315° for shutters 0–3. φ is measured from +Z toward +X; the glTF quaternions are (0, sin φ/2, 0, cos φ/2).
- In each shutter's local frame, **+Z points outward** from the heart axis and **local X is the hinge line**. The petal extends inward (local −Z) and up over the heart.

**Opening:**
- To open, rotate each shutter about its **own local +X axis by a positive angle**, starting from the closed (loaded) pose.
- In three.js: `shutter.quaternion.copy(closed).multiply(new THREE.Quaternion().setFromAxisAngle(new THREE.Vector3(1, 0, 0), angle))`, or `shutter.rotateX(angle)` from the closed pose.
- A positive angle lifts the petal tip up and swings it outward. **Recommended open angle: +100° to +110°** (the previews use +105°).
- Closed means an angle of 0.

**States:**
- Arrival / phase 1: hull, both pods, heart, closed shutters.
- A destroyed pod swaps to its `_wreck` node.
- Heart phase: open the shutters.
- Death: swap the heart for `veil_heart_wreck`, and hide the shutters or leave them open.

**Suggested hit regions** (the simulation already fixes them): pods radius 0.85 and heart radius 1.2, all on the 2.2 target plane.
