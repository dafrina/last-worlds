# Khepri terrain kit

Runtime file: `public/assets/khepri/khepri-kit.glb`.
Contract: [docs/13-khepri-world.md section 3](../../docs/13-khepri-world.md).

**The shipped file is built by `tools/blender/kit_refinement.py`** (`terrain()`, run inside Blender through the Blender MCP), which builds every piece with `tools/blender/kit_khepri.py` and then applies the 18–19 October refinement. Running `kit_khepri.py` alone, as below, exports the kit before the refinement.

## Provenance

- All geometry, vertex colours and UVs are original and generated by code in `tools/blender/kit_khepri.py`. No geometry was modelled by hand, imported, or taken from third-party assets. The GLB contains no textures or images.
- Built with Blender 5.2.2 LTS (hash `d13f752e3b9c`, bundled Python 3.13) and its bundled glTF exporter (`io_scene_gltf2` 5.2.40), headless on the Windows host. Generated 14 October 2026. Phase A (14 October) added spires, walls, fallen columns and boulders, then the alien landmarks: crystal spires, a rosette and hoodoos. Each addition left every earlier node unchanged; their decoded attributes, indices and materials are identical to the previous build.
- Determinism: a fixed seed string (`KIT_SEED`), per-piece `random.Random` streams derived from the piece name, and no time-based values. Three consecutive builds produced byte-identical GLBs.
- The textured preview (`--textures`) applies the CC0 Poly Haven maps in `art/source/polyhaven/` (`rock_face_03` on basalt, `dry_ground_rocks` on the preview ground) to preview materials only, after export; see that folder's README for their provenance.

## Rebuild

From WSL (Blender cannot read `/home` paths, so pass UNC paths from `wslpath -w`):

```bash
R=/home/davidfarina/Projects/alien-skies
cd /mnt/c && "/mnt/c/Program Files/Blender Foundation/Blender 5.2/blender.exe" --background --factory-startup \
  --python "$(wslpath -w $R/tools/blender/kit_khepri.py)" -- \
  --glb "$(wslpath -w $R/public/assets/khepri/khepri-kit.glb)" \
  --preview-dir "$(wslpath -w $R/lookdev/khepri-kit)" \
  --textures "$(wslpath -w $R/art/source/polyhaven)"
```

- Omit `--preview-dir` to export only (about 2 s).
- Omit `--textures` to skip the textured preview.
- `--engine WORKBENCH` is a fallback if EEVEE cannot start.

Every build re-parses the written GLB and fails on any contract violation. It checks node names and transforms, bounds, base depth, exact top height, triangle budgets, column count and diameter for clumps, spires and walls, materials, COLOR_0, the TEXCOORD_0 convention, forbidden content and file size. It prints the table below.

### What each piece is

- **Clumps:** 5–9 power-diagram (weighted Voronoi) columns per clump with 5–7 sides. Neighbours share near-parallel faces separated by 12–26 mm crevices. Column diameters, measured corner to corner, run 0.319–0.533 (clump 2 is the chunky one). The build fails if any clump leaves 4–9 columns or 0.28–0.55 diameter. Tops snap to two or three terrace levels so neighbouring tops merge into flat mesa blocks with a broken, stepped edge. Each clump has an 18 mm top chamfer, a few tilted or broken-stub tops, and occasional horizontal joints with thin dusty ledges. Profiles:
  - 0: plateau edge
  - 1: three-row stepped wall
  - 2: fat columns
  - 3: nine-column stepped ruin
  - 4: L-shape
  - 5: spiral staircase
- **Columns:** 0 is a jointed hexagon, 1 a broken stub (pentagon), 2 a tilted heptagon with two joints.
- **Rocks:** convex-hull basalt chunks.
  - 0: angular boulder
  - 1: fallen column segment
  - 2: tilted stump
  - 3: slab
  - 4: talus pile
  - 5: pebble scatter
- **Crystals:** clusters of 6–8 hexagonal pointed shards, splayed outward with open buried bases.
- **Spires** (3–6 columns, built like clumps but with tall, ragged, stepped tops and more joints; meant for y-scales up to about 2.5):
  - 0: five-column tower whose tops spiral down like a staircase
  - 1: twin shafts with broken low stubs at their feet
  - 2: hoodoo, a tall core ringed by lower columns
- **Walls** are elongated bands, 2–3 columns deep and 12–14 columns long, with the long axis along glTF X. **Orientation:** the back row is highest at −Z, and tops step down toward **+Z** (the front). To dress a formation edge, align X with the edge tangent and point +Z outward. Profiles:
  - 0: two-deep skyline in three height segments with two notches
  - 1: three-deep terrace stepping down to the front, with one glowing crack
  - 2: staircase descending along +X
- **Fallen columns** (long axis along X):
  - 0: a toppled hexagonal column broken in two, with rubble at the break
  - 1: a pentagonal column leaning on a rubble block, with a broken-off drum
- **Boulders:**
  - 0: a thick column drum tipped onto its shoulder, with a wedge split off
  - 1: a fractured block split along a crack, the halves leaning apart
- **Diameters:** spire and wall columns measure 0.307–0.498 corner to corner.
- **Crystal spires** (alien landmarks; `khepri_mineral` prisms on a `khepri_basalt` base, the tallest tip exactly 1.0):
  - 0: one dominant hexagonal prism with five satellites, splitting three broken column stubs
  - 1: two leaning co-dominant prisms cracking open a boulder
- **Rosette:** sixteen hexagonal columns radiating from a buried focus. Rings sit about 2°, 28° and 54° from vertical, giving a fan or flower of column ends.
- **Hoodoos:** banded, eroded shafts of stacked drums with lighter, dust-mixed sides, narrowing to a neck under a balanced, slightly tilted basalt cap.
  - 0: a single hoodoo
  - 1: a tall hoodoo with a small capped sibling

## Runtime conventions

- **Axes and seating:** +Y up. Every piece extends 0.12 (crystals 0.13–0.15) below y = 0. Clumps, columns, spires, walls, crystal spires, the rosette and hoodoos have their highest vertex at exactly y = 1.0, intended for runtime y-scaling to formation height. Fallen columns and boulders are authored at real size; do not y-scale them.
- **Hard edges:** all faces are flat-shaded, so NORMAL holds per-face normals and vertices are split per face.
- **Materials (exactly two):**
  - `khepri_basalt`: baseColorFactor 1 (default), roughness 0.92, metallic 0, single-sided. Albedo comes entirely from COLOR_0.
  - `khepri_mineral`: baseColor #2fe0d4, emissiveFactor #2fe0d4 (linear 0.028, 0.745, 0.658, core glTF with no emissive-strength extension), roughness 0.6, single-sided. Under ACES tone mapping it renders as a pale turquoise (about #87dbd6). For the exact saturated #2fe0d4, render it with `toneMapped = false` or add a glow sprite.
- **Mineral faces:** only `basalt_clump_1`, `basalt_clump_4` and `basalt_wall_1` have them. The wall has one crack plus one crust band. The crystal spires are mostly mineral, with a basalt base.
  - Two widened (50 mm) cracks per clump whose facing walls are mineral for their full height. They read as glowing seams between tops and at the crack mouths.
  - A jagged band (0.10–0.30 high) at the foot of two exterior columns, glowing on three contiguous faces plus the crack faces.
- **COLOR_0:** VEC4, normalized unsigned short, **linear** values (glTF convention; three.js GLTFLoader treats it as linear), alpha 1.
  - Basalt: dusty ochre on upward faces and ledges (one dust tone per piece, ±12% per column). Dark warm basalt sides with per-face vertical streaking. Darker base and crevices from baked ray-cast AO (64 rays, 0.42 radius, ground plane included).
  - Mineral faces: white, so the material colour is unchanged.
  - Crystal shards: grey 0.55 at the buried base to 1.0 at the tip.
- **TEXCOORD_0:** a world-scale box projection, 1 UV unit = 1 unit, with no normalisation or packing; intended for `RepeatWrapping`. The axis is chosen per face by the dominant normal component (ties go to Y, then X, then Z), in object-local glTF coordinates:
  - `|nY|` dominant (tops, ledges): `(u, v) = (X, Z)`
  - `|nX|` dominant: `(u, v) = (−sign(nX)·Z, −Y)`
  - `|nZ|` dominant: `(u, v) = (sign(nZ)·X, −Y)`

  Wall textures are upright (v grows downward, the glTF convention) and not mirrored. Seams follow face boundaries, which the flat shading already splits. UVs are object-space: when the runtime scales a clump in y by 1.2–2.2, wall textures stretch vertically by the same factor. Scale the texture repeat or use world-space triplanar mapping if that is unwanted. The build checks every exported UV against this formula from POSITION and NORMAL.
