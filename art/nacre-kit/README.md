# Nacre terrain kit

Runtime file: `public/assets/nacre/nacre-kit.glb` (1,435,600 bytes, about 477 KB gzipped; sha256 `43f4edff32bc1616da035420837ec3958cfdf6908920347c7faa07c469d67e45`).
Contract: the Nacre rows of the `KIT` table in `src/world/terrain-scatter.ts` and [docs/20-nacre-luminous-mire.md](../../docs/20-nacre-luminous-mire.md) (World section).

The kit follows the middle panel of `art/concepts/01-worlds-direction.png` (crops in `lookdev/concept/nacre-*.png`). The concept is a reference only, never a runtime asset. The kit contains:
- jagged black faceted rock with cold blue-lit tops and mossy, speckled feet;
- glowing cyan lotus caps on trumpet stems;
- clusters of glassy tubes with flared mouths;
- violet goblet flowers;
- teal lily-pad rafts;
- moss tufts with bioluminescent speckles;
- the ruins of the mire's builders.

## Provenance

- All geometry, vertex colours and UVs are original. They are generated by `tools/blender/nacre_kit.py` on top of the shared pipeline in `vesper_common.py` and `khepri_infra.py` (hard-surface builder, AO bake, box UVs, export and verification).
- No imported meshes, generated models, third-party assets or textures.
- Built with Blender 5.2.2 LTS, headless, on 16 October 2026.
- Deterministic: two consecutive builds were byte-identical, matching the hash above.

- footprints (inside the contract half extents);

- the four materials;
- COLOR_0, normals and box UVs.

## Conventions

- glTF +Y up, 1 unit = 1 game unit.
- Pivots are at the base centre, with the footprint centred on x = z = 0.
- Pieces reach y −0.12 below their pivot.
- **The camera-facing front of the ruins (orange light strips, door slots) faces +Z.** Scatter keeps ruins within ±0.45 rad of that facing.

**Materials (four).** COLOR_0 is linear and carries every face's colour, including the glowing faces:

| Material | Roughness | Covers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `nacre_rock` | 0.9 | Rock, moss and mud. Rock runs `#1e2231` → `#3b445b`; upward and sun-facing facets are lit `#7486b0` → `#a2b8e8` toward the top. Moss is `#18362f` / `#2e6650`, with violet moss `#34285a` and a mud tint at the base. AO is baked. |
| `nacre_flora` | 0.7 | Lily pads `#24604f` with a lighter rim `#4a9a7c`. |
| `nacre_ruin` | 0.6 | Ruin stone `#16181f` → `#23262f`, lit tops `#343a48`, chamfer edges `#555e74`, recesses `#08090d`, moss creeping up from the base. |
| `nacre_glow` | — | Bioluminescence and ruin lights. Under the Nacre kit look (`src/render/terrain-kit.ts`) each face emits its own COLOR_0 × 1.8 × its glow weight, and `loadNacreKit` sets the weight to the colour's brightest channel. So colour alone sets the glow, and dim teal valleys stay below the bloom threshold while white hearts and rims bloom. |

The glow colours:
- lotus caps: heart `#e8fffb`, ribs `#38b0c2`, valleys `#11606e`, rim `#6cecee`;
- stems: `#0b3a44` → `#24a8b6`;
- tubes: `#0a3a48` → `#1a8fa6`, mouths `#4ee6ea`;
- goblets: `#4a2f92` → `#8d62e8`, mouths `#d2adff`;
- speckles: `#62f4e4` and `#b48aff`;
- ruin lights: orange strips `#ff8a2a`, amber lamps `#ffb455`, cyan inlays `#40f2ee`, blue bridge panels `#4ea8ff`.

## Runtime

- `src/render/terrain-kit.ts` `loadNacreKit` loads the kit. `src/render/terrain-view.ts` `ensurePlanet('nacre')` calls it the first time Nacre streams (or comes within the route's preload range).
- Until the kit arrives, light code-built stand-ins (`src/render/nacre-kit.ts`, ≤ 500 triangles per node, same bounds and colours) draw. They are a loading fallback only, not the look.
- `src/world/terrain-nacre.ts` places the pieces.
