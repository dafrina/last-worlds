Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: three fundamentally different hero spacecraft tiers, revised design board
Primary request: Create three completely DIFFERENT original human hero fighter spacecraft for an arcade game progression. The previous idea of one ship with cosmetic upgrades is rejected. These are different chassis with radically different top silhouettes, wing plans, proportions, nose structures, engine number and layout. Their common identity comes only from practical human engineering, some worn ivory/graphite materials and cyan propulsion.
Composition: clean wide triptych with three full craft, one per equal panel on dark neutral slate studio background, same high three-quarter top view, nose pointing upward. Labels small ivory lettering: 'I — SCRAPPER', 'II — RAZOR', 'III — DOMINION'. Leave ample negative space. Whole ships visible, similar apparent gameplay scale. A small flat solid silhouette thumbnail below each reinforces that all three outlines are fundamentally different.
I SCRAPPER: cheap battered rusty frontier utility craft repurposed as fighter. Short blunt industrial fuselage, rectangular cockpit cabin, FOUR stubby almost straight wings, two large external mismatched cylindrical engine pods on side struts, visible welded repairs, rusty umber steel, faded ivory paint, crude bolt-on guns. Compact and almost tug-like but fast enough to fight; no sleek long pointed jet nose. Rugged and believable, not cute.
II RAZOR: radically different premium agile interceptor. Long narrow spear cockpit between a SPLIT TWIN-BOOM fuselage, aggressive forward-swept sickle wings, narrow rear stabilizers, THREE small tightly clustered rear exhausts, angular gun rails. Graphite/ivory surfaces, cyan engines. It is thin, predatory, fast and arrow-like; absolutely not the stub-wing utility craft from panel one.
III DOMINION: radically different maxed-out advanced assault fighter. Broad imposing flying-wing / broken-delta chassis with a small recessed central cockpit, wide armored shoulder surfaces, two separated forward weapon prongs, FOUR rectangular integrated exhaust slots across the back. The shape is a powerful wide stealth wedge, substantially wider relative to length than the Razor. Sleek layered graphite ceramic armor, selective ivory panel accents, refined recessed weapons, restrained copper mechanisms. Badass, expensive and purposeful; no gaudy gold, no giant random spikes.
Style: modern mature stylized real-time 3D concept art with strong broad forms and convincing restrained material detail, between realistic sci-fi and stylization, never toy-like or chunky voxel. Consistent lighting and visual polish. Do not turn all three into the same pointed twin-engine jet. Prioritize silhouette diversity over shared parts. No game HUD, no human faces, no franchises.
