# Strike sites: concept prompts

Twelve optional strike sites (planned 29 October 2026, not built yet). Each one is a single Blender model with a finish
per world. The concepts are references for shape, silhouette and palette only, never runtime backgrounds or model assets
(AGENTS.md invariant 9). Claude models the sites by hand from them; they are not Tripo sources.

Each prompt below is self-contained: copy one block into the image tool. Each makes **one sheet per site**, with one
panel per world it appears on, so the shared shape and the world finishes can be compared side by side. Save the result
here as `<nn>-<site>.png` and the prompt as `<nn>-<site>-prompt.txt`.

What every prompt asks for, and why:

- **The game's camera:** high three-quarter view from the south, about 55° down, looking north. The front faces the camera,
  and nothing tall stands in front of the target.
- **Scale:** about 4–6 fighter lengths across and low. Roofs stay well under the flight height, and the tallest element
  is about 2½ fighter lengths.
- **Bold silhouette:** the site must read at phone size from far off.
- **One glowing core:** the part the player shoots, clearly marked.
- **No text or logos.**

---

## 01 · Crash site (all six worlds)

```
Concept art sheet for a stylized but credible sci-fi arcade shooter, six panels in a 3x2 grid, each panel the SAME site on a different planet. Every panel uses the same camera: high three-quarter top-down view from the south, about 55 degrees down, looking north, orthographic feel, the site centered with ground around it. Mature, readable, painterly-realistic game concept, not toy-like, not photoreal. No text, labels, logos or UI.
Site: CRASH SITE. A large downed cargo freighter, broken in two and half buried in the ground at a shallow angle, a long scorched furrow ploughed behind it toward the north. Torn hull plates, exposed ribs, a spilled cargo pod. Scavengers have set up around it: two small gun turrets on sandbag-like rings, a tarp shelter, cables running into the wreck. The freighter's exposed reactor glows through a tear in the hull: this glowing reactor is the target, clearly visible from the camera.
Scale and shape: the whole site about five fighter-ship lengths across, low; the tallest piece (the raised broken bow) about two fighter lengths high. Bold silhouette readable at phone size: the long hull, the furrow, the glow.
Panels, same wreck, local finish and ground: 1 LUNA grey regolith and craters, white-grey hull, orange hazard trim. 2 KHEPRI rust-ochre dust and dark hexagonal basalt, ivory and rust steel, turquoise mineral glow nearby. 3 VESPER snowy ice shelf with blue ice cracks, graphite and white hull, cyan lights. 4 NACRE violet mire with bioluminescent plants growing over the wreck, teal glow. 5 AMNION pale cracked bone-crust ground, glowing amber yolk seeping near the hull. 6 UMBRA glossy black obsidian ground with sharp shards, faint purple glow fog.
Light: low warm sun from the upper left, shadows to the lower right.
```
