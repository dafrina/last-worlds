Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: original alien spacecraft enemy design exploration board for a 3D isometric arcade shooter
Primary request: Paint six distinct original alien spacecraft designs across THREE coherent alien world factions, two ship classes for each faction. The factions should look alien to the human swept-wing fighter, not six recolored human jets. Show both a small agile interceptor and a heavier attack craft per faction. Emphasize immediately readable top silhouettes for a portrait arcade game.
Composition: wide landscape production concept board, 3 columns and 2 rows on a uniform deep slate studio background with subtle panel gutters. Each full craft in the same elevated three-quarter top view, noses oriented upward within panels. Small neutral ivory column labels only: 'KHEPRI', 'NACRE', 'VESPER'. No stats, UI or arrows. All ship shapes remain clearly separated, with no cropped tips.
KHEPRI column: industrial alien machines adapted to dusty mineral badlands. Interceptor: asymmetric-looking but balanced three-prong wedge, dark iron and oxidized ochre armor, compact ember exhaust. Heavy craft: broad scarab-like armored flying siege machine with geometric overlapping ceramic plates and blunt twin cannon mandibles, not an actual insect.
NACRE column: unsettling biomechanical machines from luminous indigo wetlands. Interceptor: thin split crescent or manta-like chassis, dark violet iridescent ceramic, subtle teal luminous vein channels. Heavy craft: elongated petal-ring machine with smooth segmented shell and hovering internal core. Feels engineered by an alien culture, no faces, no eyes, no literal animal.
VESPER column: severe crystalline-mechanical spacecraft adapted to frozen ocean. Interceptor: narrow angular needle with offset blade fins, pale cool ceramic and black joints. Heavy craft: wide fractured diamond frame with separate radiator wings, charcoal understructure, controlled orange power channels.
Style: mature, cool modern stylized 3D hard-surface concept art with convincing materials and strong clean silhouettes, restrained surface detail. Neither ultra-realistic nor toy-like. Lighting crisp and sculptural. Emissive details restrained so real game enemy projectiles can still dominate readability. Different wing plans, engine arrangements and proportions across all six. No named franchises, no human pilot, no decorative stars, no giant guns obscuring the entire shape.
Purpose: authored enemy archetype families suitable for later bounded modular assembly; not arbitrary random geometry. Do not draw insects or animals, these are flying combat machines.
