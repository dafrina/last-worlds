No human cockpit, no familiar jet nose, no conventional paired wings, no animal faces.
Composition: wide three-panel production concept board on dark slate studio background, full objects seen from a consistent high three-quarter angle. Small ivory labels: 'ORRERY', 'SHARD', 'VEIL'. One large design per panel, plenty of breathing room, each visible without cropping. Show sculptural 3D volume; all feel like they belong in the same mature isometric arcade game.
Left ORRERY: a floating ring-based gravitic war machine. Three offset segmented dark bronze / obsidian rings interlocked around a compact suspended glowing amber polyhedral core, like an alien gyroscope. Some deliberate negative space through the structure, tapered emitter arms mounted onto ring segments, no wings. Broad clean ring sections, sparse glyph-like inlays, restrained glow.
Middle SHARD: a violently asymmetric crystalline-mechanical interceptor. A long off-center black-ceramic spine with five unequal pale iridescent blade plates suspended around it in a deliberately directional spiral arrangement, angular negative spaces, thin orange field connections. Visually a fractured arrowhead rather than a symmetrical jet, lethal precise silhouette. No decorative debris cloud; every blade is an intentional part of the ship.
Right VEIL: a fluid biomechanical hunter made of a dark violet pearlescent central seed hull with three broad swept ribbon-like armored fins looping backward into an open lattice, restrained luminous teal vein channels and one concealed aft propulsion cavity. Elegant alien engineering with organic curvature, not a literal jellyfish or insect, no tentacle fringe or soft cartoon skin. Smooth hard surfaces, coherent large shapes.
Style: beautiful sculptural modern stylized 3D game concept art, credible hard surfaces and selective organic structure, deep studio lighting, broad readable geometry, neither hyperreal military model nor cute toy. No humans, planets, stars, interface widgets or named franchises. Strong diversity: circular/radial, angular/asymmetric, flowing/organic. These are enemy concept candidates, not a demand for complex random procedural topology.
