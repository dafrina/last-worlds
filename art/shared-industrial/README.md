# Shared industrial kit (all planets)

## Provenance

- All geometry, masks and UVs are original and generated by `tools/blender/shared_industrial.py`. No hand modelling, imported meshes or third-party assets are used, and the GLB contains no textures.
- The script imports helper code from `khepri_kit.py` and `khepri_infra.py` in the same folder, with bytecode writing disabled.
- Built with Blender 5.2.2 LTS (hash `d13f752e3b9c`) and its bundled glTF exporter (`io_scene_gltf2` 5.2.40), headless on the Windows host, on 14 October 2026.
- Determinism: fixed seed (`SEED`), no time-based values. Three consecutive builds were byte-identical, matching the hash above.
- The previews use CC0 Poly Haven maps from `art/source/polyhaven/` (`metal_plate_02` on the kit, `dry_ground_rocks` on the ground) as luminance detail and normals, applied after export.

```bash
  --python "$(wslpath -w $R/tools/blender/shared_industrial.py)" -- \
  --glb "$(wslpath -w $R/public/assets/shared/industrial-kit.glb)" \
  --preview-dir "$(wslpath -w $R/art/shared-industrial)" \
  --textures "$(wslpath -w $R/art/source/polyhaven)"
```

Omit `--preview-dir` to export only (about 3 s).

Every build re-parses its GLB and fails on any violation of these checks:
- exactly the 14 node names, with no node transforms;
- exactly three materials, and no textures, cameras, animations or extensions;
- triangle budgets, the bounds rules below, and everything at most 2.0 tall;
- COLOR_0 and TEXCOORD_0 present, with UVs checked per triangle against the box projection;
- **pipe snapping:**
  - every open end lies on its cell edge with the flange centred on the centreline;
  - no geometry leaves the 2 × 2 cell, so there are no doubled flanges;
  - all open ends of a diameter class share one identical profile of position, normal and mask. The build reports "class s: 7 open ends share 1 profile, class l: 9 open ends share 1 profile".

## Pipe grid conventions

- **Cells:** pipes live on a 2-unit grid. The pivot is the cell centre on the ground, and every open end lies on a cell edge at centreline height 0.35. The flange outer radius is 0.115 for small pipes and 0.215 for large ones.
- **Native orientation:**
  - Straight: runs along Z from −1 to +1.
  - Elbow: enters at Z = −1 travelling along Z and exits at X = +1 travelling along X.
  - Tee: runs along Z from −1 to +1 with a branch exiting at X = +1.

  Rotate in 90° steps about Y. The elbow joins the Z = −1 edge to the X = +1 edge; rotating it by 90° steps joins any pair of adjacent edges.
- **Joints:** each end carries a half-flange (0.035 thick for small pipes, 0.045 for large) ending exactly on the cell edge, so two pieces form one full flange. The flat end faces meet back to back inside the joint, so they stay hidden while a terminal end still looks closed.
- **Supports:** place a support at the centre of a straight cell, so its footprint sits under the pipe body and clear of the flanges. One large pipe rests on the beam. Two small pipes can run side by side at ±0.18 from the grid line and rest on the saddles.

## Planet-neutral shading

- **Materials:** exactly three, all single-sided.
  - `shared_metal` (structure): base grey 0.5, metallic 0.6, roughness 0.5.
  - `shared_paint` (flanges, bands, valve body, containers, saddles): base grey 0.62, metallic 0, roughness 0.6.
  - `shared_light` (lamps): white emissive.
  
  The runtime replaces their colours from the planet palette.
- **COLOR_0 is a mask set, not colour:** VEC4, normalized unsigned short, alpha 1.
  - **R** is baked ambient occlusion (48-ray hemisphere, 0.35 radius, ground plane included): 1 open, 0 occluded. Pipes are baked as if a straight neighbour were attached at each open end. Every seam vertex takes its AO from one canonical straight-pipe scene, so masks match across any joint.
  - **G** is edge wear: 1 on chamfers and flange rims, 0.55 on flange steps, 0 elsewhere.
  - **B** is dust and grime: high on upward faces, in occluded upward corners and in a band near the ground, fading with height. Pipe pieces normalise height by the flange top, so seams match.
  - Lamp faces carry (1, 0, 0).
  
  Loaded with stock glTF materials, the masks would tint the surface as colour. The runtime is expected to compose colour instead: `base → mix(wear, G) → mix(dust, B) → × AO(R)`.
- **TEXCOORD_0:** the world-scale box projection used by the planet kits (see `art/khepri-kit/README.md`), for tiled `metal_plate_02` detail. Pipe UVs shift by exactly 2.0 between adjacent cells.

## Previews

The previews compose the masks exactly as above:
- Khepri palette: graphite metal, weathered ochre paint, ochre dust, amber lamps.
- Alternate palette: icy pale metal, blue paint, frost, cool blue lamps.

Lighting and camera match the planet kits: ACES 1.3 at +0.81 EV, warm sun from the upper left and slightly behind, orthographic camera at 55° elevation with zero yaw.

- `preview-kit.png`: every piece in the Khepri palette, plus an icy swatch of five pieces on a pale slab. The same meshes and masks with only the palette swapped show that the masks theme other planets.
- `preview-pipeline.png`: a 900×1600 view at game scale. A large pipeline runs on the grid:
  - straights on supports, the valve, a tee branching to an elbow into a tank farm, and an elbow turning the main run;
  - a pair of small runs on shared supports;
  - a single small line with a tee, a straight, an elbow and branch straights;
  - containers and crate stacks;
  - an icy-palette swatch at the top.

  Every joint shows one full flange, with no gaps or doubled rings.
