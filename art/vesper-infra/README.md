## Provenance

- All geometry, vertex colours and UVs are original and generated by `tools/blender/vesper_infra.py`, using the shared pipeline in `vesper_common.py`.
- No imported meshes, third-party assets or textures.
- Built with Blender 5.2.2 LTS and its bundled glTF exporter (5.2.40), headless, on 15 October 2026.
- Deterministic: three builds were byte-identical, matching the hash above.

## Rebuild

```bash
R=/home/davidfarina/Projects/alien-skies
cd /mnt/c && "/mnt/c/Program Files/Blender Foundation/Blender 5.2/blender.exe" --background --factory-startup \
  --python "$(wslpath -w $R/tools/blender/vesper_infra.py)" -- \
  --glb "$(wslpath -w $R/public/assets/vesper/vesper-infra.glb)" \
  --preview-dir "$(wslpath -w $R/art/vesper-infra)"
```

The build exports to a temporary file, re-parses it, and only publishes when every check passes:
- names, footprints, bases, heights and budgets (≤ 3,000; the radiator tile ≤ 600);
- materials;
- COLOR_0 and box UVs;
- **tiling seams:** the radiator array along x and the pier along z, where every seam vertex has a twin with identical position, normal and colour one period away.

## Conventions

glTF +Y up, 1 unit = 1 game unit, front +Z, base-centre pivots.
- **Land pieces:** lowest point y = −0.05.
- **Water pieces:** the pier segment and the icebreaker have their **waterline at y = 0**.

**Materials (two):**
- `vesper_ceramic` (roughness 0.45, metallic 0.05): albedo from COLOR_0. The palette is black ceramic `#15181b` / `#0d0f11`, grey ceramic `#2b3138`, steel `#454c54`, pale ceramic `#d3dae1` accents, a little orange paint and dark visors.
- `vesper_glow`: orange #ff7a26, emissive; COLOR_0 is white on these faces.

COLOR_0 is linear and bakes:
- ray-cast AO with a ground plane for land pieces, and edge wear;
- a light frost dusting on upward faces and near the ground (snow-dusted, but the ceramic stays black).

The tiling pieces bake AO against their neighbouring copies, with no random variation. UVs are the world-scale box projection (1 UV = 1 unit).
